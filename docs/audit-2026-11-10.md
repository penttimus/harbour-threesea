# Audit of 2026-11-10 (security, speed, unwanted behaviour)

A small audit, sized to cost little: not a second pass over the whole tree, which the audit of
2026-11-04 covered.

What was read:

- everything on `main` since the audit's fixes (`audit-fixes..main`: the Secrets prompt, the
  chat list's reloads, drafts, the call remorse, the vibration-only notification, the systemd
  unit), line by line;
- the two fix branches not merged yet, `fix/earpiece-hang` and `fix/camera-deadlock`;
- the entry points from outside the app again: `bridge/dbusinterface.cpp` in full, and the
  share folder in `core/src/app_media.rs` (ADR 0031);
- `cargo audit`.

What was **not** done: `scripts/check.sh`, the SDK tests, anything on the emulator or a phone, and
the rest of the core, bridge and QML. Findings 2 and 4 to 6 are fixed and 1 is decided (see each); 3 is open.

## Findings

### 1. `cargo audit` fails: RUSTSEC-2026-0459 in the bundled SQLite (security, medium by CVSS; low for us)

`libsqlite3-sys` 0.35.0 (through `rusqlite` 0.37, `bundled-sqlcipher`). SQLite before 3.54.0 can
return uninitialised memory of the process when a database is opened with a crafted `-wal` file
of another page size. We use WAL (`core/src/store.rs:506`).

- Exposure: the one database the core opens is in the app's own data folder, so the files would
  have to be planted there by something running as the user, which can do worse already; and
  what leaks goes into the app's own query results, not back to whoever planted the files. No
  path from the network was found. Whether SQLCipher's page check rejects such a file first was
  not established.
- The fix cargo-audit suggests does not fix it: the advisory itself says `libsqlite3-sys`
  0.39.0/0.39.1 with `bundled-sqlcipher` still bundle an affected SQLite (3.53.4, through
  `sqlcipher-src`). Updating would turn the check green and leave the bug in.
- Consequence today: `scripts/check.sh` stops at "cargo audit", and so do CI on every push and
  the weekly `advisories.yml`.
- To decide (§7.7): wait for a `libsqlite3-sys`/`sqlcipher-src` on SQLite ≥ 3.54 and until then
  ignore this one advisory in `.cargo/audit.toml` with the reason written beside it; or build
  SQLCipher from a patched source ourselves.

**Decided** (user, 2026-11-10): passed over in `.cargo/audit.toml` until a fixed bundle exists,
ADR 0035. The fault itself stays until then.

### 2. After a pipeline that did not stop, the call engine is used again (bug, `fix/camera-deadlock`)

`CallEngine::stop()` now gives up after 3 s and leaves the pipeline behind. Only the signals of
`m_webrtc` and `m_channel` are disconnected; the pad probes and `new-sample`/`deep-element-added`
handlers of that pipeline (`callengine.cpp` 147, 160, 204, 478, 508–520, 689, 765) still carry
`this`, and the engine lives as long as the app (`callmanager.cpp:35`). If the left-behind
pipeline's threads go on, they reach the engine during the next call: the capture probe feeds the
new call's echo canceller from two threads, and frames of the old pipeline can be posted into the
new call. Same pattern in `GroupCallEngine`.

Also in that state the camera or the microphone source may stay open until the app ends (a bin
stops its sinks first, so nothing should still be sent, but the camera stays busy and the
system may show it in use), and hanging up freezes the UI for up to 6 s (camera branch, then
pipeline).

Suggestion: after a stop that timed out, give the next call a new engine object and leave the old
one behind with its pipeline (or refuse calls until the app is started again), and say so in the
log. To be confirmed on the phone that showed the hang.

**Fixed** on `fix/camera-deadlock`: such an engine keeps its canceller, refuses another call and
is left behind for good; the manager makes a new one. Not yet built in the SDK. The camera or
microphone staying open and the wait of up to 6 s are as before.

### 3. Leaving the chat during the call remorse may start the call at once (unwanted behaviour, to verify)

`ChatPage.qml` `startCallWithRemorse` (pull request 4). As far as remembered, Silica runs a
pending remorse action when its page goes away; the Silica sources were not at hand to check.
If so, someone who pulled "Call" by mistake and swipes back to get away rings the other side
immediately, which is the opposite of what the remorse is for. Check on the emulator **without
a real contact** (a call rings them); if it holds, cancel `callRemorse` when the page is left.

### 4. The chat list reloads up to 20 times a second in the background (speed, battery)

Since 2026-11-07 `ConversationsPage` reloads on every event also under other pages and with the
window closed (wanted: no jump on coming back), and since `chatPreviewChanged` also 700 ms after
each pause in typing. The only brake is the 50 ms timer (`ConversationsPage.qml:130`). While the
servers hand out what they kept after a login, each message means a `conversations` query, its
JSON across the FFI and a model update, for a list nobody sees: with the phone waking for
messages overnight this is paid on every wake-up.

Suggestion: keep 50 ms while the page is seen, and a second or two while it is not. The list is
still current before anyone can look at it, so nothing jumps.

**Fixed:** 50 ms while seen, 1.5 s otherwise, and a reload still due runs the moment the list
comes into sight. Not yet run on the emulator.

### 5. A hung earpiece pipeline can still be inside `onNeedData` (bug, minor, `fix/earpiece-hang`)

`EarpiecePlayer::release()` disconnects the handlers and, when the pipeline does not stop,
goes on to wipe and clear `m_data` and reset `m_offset`. Disconnecting does not wait for a
handler that is running: a streaming thread inside `onNeedData` at that moment reads `m_data`
while the GUI thread changes it. The window is small and only exists once the decoder hangs;
the leaked copy keeps the bytes it already wrapped valid. A mutex around `m_data`/`m_offset` in
the two callbacks and in `release()` closes it.

**Fixed** on `fix/earpiece-hang`: the lock, and `release()` now keeps the buffer the pipeline
really reads when it hangs (after a seek or speed change in raw mode it kept a copy instead);
those two paths wipe the old bytes once the new start has its own. Not yet built in the SDK.

### 6. The sweep of shared copies counts folders it cannot remove (minor)

`shared_folders_in` takes every entry of `/tmp` whose name starts with our prefix. One that
another process made (and that we may not delete) blocks sharing once it is large
(`shared_size_in`) and keeps the 30 s sweeper running, with a warning each round. Only a local
process can do this, and only as a nuisance. Skipping entries not owned by our user ends it.

**Fixed:** only folders of our own user count (rustix's `process` feature for `geteuid`; no new
crate), with a test.

## Looked at and found in order

- `dbusinterface.cpp`: tokens from `/dev/urandom`, compared in full, single-use, expiring; the
  methods without a token go through the window's lock guard.
- The share folder: tmpfs only, random name, `0700`/`0600`, `create_new`, all-or-nothing,
  removed at the deadline by the wall clock.
- The Secrets prompt: the retry happens once and only without a visible window; no path to a
  key written or logged.
- Drafts: written 700 ms after a change and only when changed.
